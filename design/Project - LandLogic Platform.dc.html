LandLogic Platform

Creating a user-friendly platform.

Summary

LandLogic's platform needed an overhaul. It worked, but flows were clunky, difficult to understand and unintuitive. As Creative Director of LandLogic, I took on the project to update key flows, revise page design, and refine critical interactions. Of course, that meant I had to learn Figma and massively improve my UX/UI skills!

Milestones
Define strategic goals Analyze weak spots Iterate on options Refine design
*
All mockups represent real, existing assets.
Understanding the user

The platform was already active, but leadership felt it needed improvements to both the design and experience. I specialize in branding design, not UX/UI, but this was the perfect opportunity to hone my skills. This was the most detailed UX/UI project I had undertaken.

I started with getting a full understanding of the existing design, why decisions were made, where the weak spots were, and what the goals for the platform were.

I then studied the user research, user profiles and existing feedback on the platform. I worked very closely with leadership, development, and UX/UI teams to scope the project, set timelines and set expectations.

Working through revisions

The overall objective was to simplify the user experience to make it easier to get to a certain goal, such as saving a project or downloading a report.

I worked on concepts, flows and updated UX and UI to pitch to the team. I ran brainstorms, testing workshops and feedback sessions to ensure we were all aligned on functionality and design. I also cleaned up the key elements of the design system like typography, colour, icons, accessibility, etc.

My experience in Figma was limited, so I put in some hefty self-teaching time. I didn't need to be a Figma expert, but had to make my files complete and consistent enough with proper formatting, styles, and components to be able to hand it over to the development team successfully.

Key objectives
Reduce number of clicks Simplify data visualization Clarify information design Simplify process from A to B
